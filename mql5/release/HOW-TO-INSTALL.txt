GoldCycle - the gold bot inside MetaTrader 5
============================================
Use a DEMO account first.

BEFORE YOU START
- If the old GoldCycle bot (the browser one) is installed on this computer, stop it:
  open "GoldCycle Manager" and choose 3. Never run two bots on the same account.

INSTALL (once)
1. Open MetaTrader 5 (Exness) and log in to your account.
2. Top menu: File > Open Data Folder. Open the folder MQL5, then Experts.
3. Copy the file GoldCycle.ex5 into that Experts folder.
4. Go back to MetaTrader. Press Ctrl+N (Navigator). Right-click "Expert Advisors" > Refresh.
   "GoldCycle" appears.
5. In Market Watch, right-click gold (XAUUSDm or XAUUSD) > Chart Window. Make the window big.
6. Drag GoldCycle onto that gold chart.
7. Tab "Common": tick "Allow Algo Trading".
   Tab "Inputs": "Automated testing only" must stay false. Press OK.
8. Click "Algo Trading" at the top of MetaTrader so it is GREEN.

The panel appears on the chart. It should say DEMO and show no red message.

USE
Point = 1.00 of price (4000 to 4003 is 3 points). Start with lot 0.01.

SINGLE tab (one trade)
- BUY now / SELL now: opens at once, without TP and SL.
- Or type a trigger price, TP and SL (both optional) > "Start at trigger price".
- "Close trade" closes it.

CYCLE tab
- Choose BUY or SELL, type the trigger price, lot and TP points > "Start cycle".
  1) When price touches the trigger, order 1 opens with a TP and NO stop loss.
  2) When order 1 reaches its TP, order 2 opens at once, with its stop loss at order 1's entry.
  3) When order 2 hits its stop loss, the next cycle waits at the entry again (plus the gap, if set).
- IMPORTANT: order 1 has no stop loss. Always set a "Stop price" and/or a "Daily loss" limit.

CHANGE TP / SL OF AN OPEN TRADE (Single and Cycle)
- Under "Change THIS open trade": type the prices > "Set TP / SL on this trade".
- Or drag the TP / SL lines on the chart. An empty box removes it.

STOP
- "Stop now": the bot stops, the open trade stays open.
- "Close all and stop" (red): closes the trade and stops. Ctrl+Shift+K closes both bots.
- Remove the bot: right-click the chart > Expert List > GoldCycle > Remove.

GOOD TO KNOW
- MetaTrader must stay OPEN and the computer ON, or the bot does nothing.
  TP and SL stay on Exness even if MetaTrader is closed.
- After closing and opening MetaTrader, press "Resume" to continue.
- If you close a bot trade by hand, that bot stops.
- Use the bot on ONE gold chart only.
- Gold is closed on weekends and for one hour every night (03:00-04:00 Pakistan time).
